The Problem

Information is scattered. Seconds are lost.

During a crisis, emergency calls flood disjointed hotlines, WhatsApp groups, social media, and agency radios. Triage is manual, duplicates slip through, and volunteers nearby never even hear the call.

⚠️ The "golden hour" problem

By the time a report reaches the right responder, minutes — sometimes hours — have passed. Aapat Setu collapses that gap to seconds by having AI triage every report the moment it lands.

Features

One platform. Every stakeholder.

From the moment a citizen hits "send" to the moment the incident is resolved, every step is tracked, verified, and coordinated live.

📱

1-Tap Incident Reporting

Citizens pin the location on a live map, describe what they see, and submit. Geolocation auto-fills; optional photos attach proof.

🤖

AI Triage Engine AI

Every report is instantly verified, summarized, priority-scored (low/medium/high/critical), and cross-checked for duplicates — before any human sees it.

🗺️

Shared Live Map

All responders and volunteers see the same color-coded map. Critical incidents pulse red; resolved ones fade green. No refresh needed.

🎯

Responder Command Center

Agencies see incoming reports, verify with one click, assign resources, broadcast public alerts, and view analytics — all from a single dashboard.

🤝

Volunteer Dispatch

Trained volunteers see open tasks sorted by distance, claim them with one tap, and post live updates from the scene.

📡

Real-Time Updates

WebSockets push new incidents, status changes, and public alerts to every connected user instantly — no reload, no delay.

📢

Public Alert Broadcasts

Agencies can push safety alerts (evacuations, weather warnings, road closures) to all users in one click.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Charts break incidents down by type, area, priority, and response time to help agencies deploy resources smarter next time.

🔐

Role-Based Access (JWT)

Secure JWT authentication for citizens, volunteers, and responders — everyone sees only the screens and actions meant for them.

The Workflow

From emergency to resolution — in seconds.

The judge-demo narrative in one clean pipeline:

Citizen reports

A flood is pin-dropped on the map and described in seconds.

AI verifies AI

Spam and vague reports are filtered out automatically.

AI summarizes AI

A clean one-line summary is generated for responders.

AI prioritizes AI

Critical / High / Medium / Low scored from keywords + incident type.

Duplicate check AI

Location + description similarity prevents duplicate work.

Nearest teams alerted

Responders and volunteers nearby get it pushed live on their dashboards.

Volunteer claims

A nearby volunteer taps "Claim Task" and heads to the location.

Citizen updated live

Every status change is tracked in a timeline the citizen can follow.
